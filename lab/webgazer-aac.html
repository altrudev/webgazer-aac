Experimental eye-tracking diagnostic. Your camera starts only after you press Start. Camera frames and gaze coordinates are processed in this browser. Technical error details
WebGazer 3.5.3 is version-pinned and integrity-checked. MediaPipe FaceMesh runtime assets are fetched from a version-pinned third-party CDN; AAC adapter code is hosted on Altru.dev.
Tracking targets
